# App Store Listing (English)

## Name

PowerView

## Subtitle

See where your battery went

## Promotional Text

Turn an iPhone sysdiagnose into a clear, day-by-day battery report. Find the apps and habits that drain your battery, all processed on your device.

## Description

Ever wondered why your battery ran out early? Your iPhone already keeps a detailed power log. PowerView turns it into a clear report you can read.

Capture a sysdiagnose, share it to PowerView, and get up to a month of battery history, day by day. Every number comes from the system's own power log, so you see what actually happened instead of an estimate.

EVERY DAY, EXPLAINED
• Battery used, time on battery, screen-on time and total energy for each day
• Standby and in-use drain rates, and how long a full charge would last at that pace
• Highlights that point out what cost the most battery, compared with your usual days

FIND WHAT DRAINS IT
• Energy used by each app and system process, split into on-screen and background use
• Energy by component: processor, display, Wi-Fi, cellular modem and more
• Apps that iOS flagged for heavy background CPU or disk use

HOUR BY HOUR
• The battery level through the day, with charging, screen-on periods and setting changes marked
• Battery temperature, with peaks called out
• Lanes for screen time, Always-On Display, audio, notifications, wakes from sleep, Wi-Fi and cellular use

WHAT WAS ON SCREEN
• A timeline of which app was on screen, minute by minute
• Screen brightness in nits and ambient light, so you can see when brightness cost you

NOTIFICATIONS AND NETWORK
• Which apps sent the most notifications, and how many woke the phone
• Wi-Fi and cellular data in 15-minute steps, signal bars, and every switch between 5G and 4G

CHARGING HABITS
• When you charge, how long each session lasts and how long the battery sits at 100%
• Typical start and end levels, overnight charging, and wired or wireless chargers

STABILITY
• Crashes, hangs, memory kills and resource limit reports from the same sysdiagnose, grouped by day and by app

COMPARE ANY TWO DAYS
• Line up two days' battery curves, from the time you unplugged or by time of day
• See every figure side by side to find out what changed

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
A sysdiagnose contains personal data, so PowerView processes it entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Your reports are never uploaded and never leave your device. No account, no tracking.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Press and hold both volume buttons and the side button for about 1.5 seconds to start a sysdiagnose.
2. After about 10 minutes, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Analytics Data.
3. Find the file that starts with "sysdiagnose_", tap Share and choose PowerView.

PowerView walks you through these steps in the app. The power log keeps about a month of battery levels, about two weeks of hourly records, and the last day or two of second-by-second detail, so capture soon after the day you want to look at.
